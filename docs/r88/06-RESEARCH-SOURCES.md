# Research ledger

Dates below are observation dates unless a report year is explicitly named. No traffic, revenue, salary or total-market estimate was inferred from a marketing page. Source summaries are paraphrases.

### IR-01 - Iran member report to the 2025 WAHO conference

Source: https://www.waho.org/iran/

Observed: 2026-10-03. The 2025 Arabian registry report states approximately 1,000-1,200 registered foals per year. This is breed-specific annual registration flow, not all horses, all breeders, platform customers, or total addressable revenue.

Evidence: Primary registry report; indexed text available; direct open intermittently failed.

### IR-02 - Sheyhe published product

Source: https://sheyhe.com/

Observed: 2026-10-03. Advertises bilingual horse and pedigree information, offspring and results, horse listings and equipment. Product overlap is substantial; its user count, transaction volume and revenue were not verified.

Evidence: Primary competitor product page.

### IR-03 - Chaharnal published product

Source: https://chaharnal.com/

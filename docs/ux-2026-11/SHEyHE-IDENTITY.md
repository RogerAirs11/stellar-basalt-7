# Sheyhe identity reconciliation

**1,369 previously unlinked public profiles now point to 1,369 distinct active registry horses.** This resolves 11.2% of the 12,225 source-only profiles. They were existing horses, not 1,369 new registrations. No official identity, pedigree, date, sex, chip, coat or ownership record was changed.

| Supabase readback | Before | After | Change |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Linked Sheyhe profiles | 23,258 | 24,627 | +1,369 |
| Unlinked profiles | 12,225 | 10,856 | −1,369 |
| Published source notes | 44,826 | 47,473 | +2,647 |
| Active registry horses with notes | 22,730 | 24,098 | +1,368 |

**What visitors gain:** 1,354 notes on use at the time of publication, 1,238 on the country listed by the source, and 55 on the listed province. One newly linked horse had no publishable additional field. No new coat, strain or birth-year gap note qualified. A source's listed province is not a current location. The notes are attributed and dated, not official registrations.

**Why the records were linked:** 1,225 had a unique chip plus a unique UEL; 56 had a unique chip plus a unique registration number; 88 had a unique chip plus a spelling-equivalent Persian name and supporting sex/year or parent evidence. The dual-identifier rule also requires corroborating records and rejects weak name agreement. Known conflicts, ambiguous assignments and pre-claimed registry horses were excluded. No link rests on a name alone, and no parent was inferred. Of 1,651 distinct candidate pairs across the two initial rules, 219 lacked sufficient name/family corroboration and 63 failed a conflict or other identifier guard.

**Archive versus current page:** every one of the 47,473 published values was independently compared with the pinned archive. All 44,826 previous notes retained the identical digest. Twenty live source pages were sampled, not 1,369; all confirmed the sampled identifier checks, and 20 newly displayed fields also matched the current pages. Totals across releases: 124 page-rechecked fields on 57 horses. No notes collided with official values or appeared on an ineligible horse in the database audit. Numeric identifiers are not exposed by the public evidence labels.

**Separate racing-site frontier:** at its recorded status check, Asbdavani had 276 pages checked, 14,669 pending and 4 held; 82 cumulative career repairs. Forty-four indexed racing horses still lacked a verified registry link. These are separate from the Sheyhe totals.

**Validation scope:** both frontend type checks, production build and assets, 24 targeted tests, 10 responsive browser journeys, and public-evidence fixtures passed. This is not a new full-suite pass or a claim of complete private-registry parity. See `RUNBOOK.md` for rules, refresh order and rollback.
